Our journey

2012 — Company founded

Started under the name INCH Modern Technology with a focus on practical construction solutions.

2019 — Rebranded

Expanded into laboratory construction and installations, leading to the rebrand as Cladding Investments.

2020 — Service expansion

Broadened offerings to include electrical, plumbing, kitchen modernization and general equipment supply.

2021 — Industry recognition

Gained recognition for high-quality work in educational and institutional facilities.

2025 — Continued growth

Continuing to expand our reach and strengthen our reputation across Zambia’s construction sector.
